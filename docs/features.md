# Features

Everything runs in your local Python kernel.

<div class="quantui-landing" markdown="0">
<div class="section" style="padding-top: 0;">
<div class="features-grid features-grid--page">

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">⚗️</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">Molecule Input</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    Paste XYZ, pick from ~1,900 bundled molecules, search by name, SMILES or CAS
    (works offline), or upload a file. Edit bonds, angles and atoms in place.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">🔬</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">3D Visualization</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    Molecules, trajectories, vibrations and orbitals render inline with
    py3Dmol, <strong>fully offline</strong>.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">⚡</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">Calculations</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    HF, nine DFT functionals, MP2, CCSD and CCSD(T). Single points, optimizations,
    <strong>transition states</strong> and <strong>IRC</strong>, frequencies, UV-Vis, NMR,
    PES scans. PCM solvent.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">📊</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">Spectra &amp; Analysis</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    IR, UV-Vis and NMR spectra, point groups, orbital diagrams,
    density and ESP surfaces, thermochemistry, side-by-side comparisons.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">📂</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">Exports &amp; History</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    Every run auto-saves and replays later. Export XYZ, Molden, cube,
    trajectories, a <code class="inline-code">.zip</code> bundle or a standalone
    <code class="inline-code">.py</code> script.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">🚀</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">GPU Acceleration</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    Optional NVIDIA offload via
    <a class="hero__link" href="https://github.com/pyscf/gpu4pyscf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">gpu4pyscf</a>
    for HF, DFT and TD-DFT. Each result shows which device ran it.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">📈</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">Time Estimates</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    Per-machine predictions before each run, calibrated on your
    hardware and checked against actual run times.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">⌨️</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">CLI &amp; Analytics</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    <code class="inline-code">quantui</code> launches the app, checks the GPU,
    tails the log and builds an HTML usage dashboard. See the <a href="CLI.md">CLI reference</a>.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">🗂️</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">Cluster Batch Jobs</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    <code class="inline-code">quantui-batch</code> submits calculations to
    SLURM from a login node: presets, chained jobs, reruns and result summaries.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">🖥️</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">Voil&agrave; App Mode</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    A widget-only app for classrooms and research groups, with light and
    dark themes.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <div class="feature-card__icon">🧲</div>
  <div class="feature-card__title">Coordination Complexes</div>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    14 bundled metal complexes, basis and spin-state checks before each run,
    and optional GFN-FF pre-optimization.
  </p>
</div>

</div>
</div>
